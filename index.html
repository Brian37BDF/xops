Esc

Needs a keyboard and mouse. The mouse is captured when you press Start and stays captured through menus and missions. Esc releases it; click the game to capture it again. While it's released, a second Esc reaches the game. Mission text switches between an English translation and the original Japanese. Completed missions get a check mark in the mission list.
